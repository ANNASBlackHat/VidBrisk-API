The octopus is one of the strangest anomalies in the entire ocean. It has three hearts, blue blood, and can edit its own genetic code. It can taste with its skin, regrow lost limbs, and change color faster than you can blink. Yet, most astonishing of all is its intelligence. You probably already know that the octopus can learn how to solve mazes and is the only invertebrate to use tools, but it goes much deeper. They are so intelligent scientists believe they may even be conscious, aware of themselves and their place in the world.
