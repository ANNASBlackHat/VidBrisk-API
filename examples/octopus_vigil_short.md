# The Deep Sea Vigil: The 53-Month Mother

About fourteen hundred meters beneath the surface, on a dark rock ledge in Monterey Canyon, a deep-sea octopus guards her eggs.

Researchers find her. They log the coordinates. And they return.

She hasn't moved.

Months turn into years. Four and a half years pass in freezing darkness.

She never leaves. She never eats.

After fifty-three months, the eggs finally hatch. Her young drift away into the ocean.

She did not survive. But her watch was finished.
