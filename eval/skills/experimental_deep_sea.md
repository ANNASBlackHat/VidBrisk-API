# Cinematic Maritime & Naval Exploration — Experimental Style Skill
version: 2.0

## Tone & Visual Atmosphere
- Highly atmospheric, cinematic, desaturated color grading.
- Priority: authentic underwater wreckage exploration, deep-sea ROV camera feeds, sonar scans, and stormy ocean swells.

## Retrieval & Keyword Strategy
- Emphasize movement and motion terms: "slow tracking shot", "panning across rusted hull", "descending camera submersible".
- Reject generic static stock footage (calm blue surface water, sunny tropical beach).
- Favor specific maritime historical terminology: "scuttled fleet", "scapa flow anchor chain", "depth charge blast", "heavy steel hull".
